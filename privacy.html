Privacy Policy — Jack Reading RPG Drive

Last updated: 2026-10-20

What the app accesses

The app requests only the Google Drive scope drive.file. With it, the app can see and change only the Drive files and folders that the app created or that the user selected for it. It cannot see other files in the user's Drive.

How data is used

The app stores and reads game image files and folders in the owner's own Drive for a personal reading game. A record of file IDs and checksums is kept on the owner's private server.

Sharing

No data is sold or shared with third parties. Google user data is not used for advertising or model training.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
